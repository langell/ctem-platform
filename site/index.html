CTEM

Continuous exposure management. Scope what you own, discover exposures, prioritize by risk, validate what is real, mobilize the fix.

Who

Security and platform teams who already run scans and need one API in front of assets, findings, policy, and the scan kick.

The billable unit is a scan kick. Usage is readable from the meters API.

Loop

  1. Scope Assets you own are the scope.
  2. Discover Discovery records exposures on those assets.
  3. Prioritize Findings are ordered by risk.
  4. Validate Validation checks which findings are real.
  5. Mobilize Policy mobilizes the fix.
